Lucky Sevens

Quick Game Guide

Learn the rules, rewards, and special events to make the most of every spin. This guide is designed for quick reference while you play.

How to Play

  1. You start with 125 coins.
  2. Adjust your bet using the + and - buttons.
  3. Press PLAY to spin the three reels.
  4. If you land a winning combination, coins are awarded automatically.

Special Events

If you land two or more rubies, the game triggers a special win: it may save a screenshot of the play and record additional win details.

Big wins may also generate a notification so you don’t miss your reward.

Helpful Tips

  • Keep a balanced bet to extend your play session.
  • Check the leaderboard to compare your progress.
  • Don’t forget to claim your daily reward when you log in.
  • Use music and sound effects to better recognize big wins.

Final tip: if you see repeated rubies, stay alert... a big win might be coming.
